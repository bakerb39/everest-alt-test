01 · The problem

Two curves are crossing, and almost everyone is building for only one of them.

~50%

U.S. adults are lonelier than ever. In 2023 the Surgeon General declared loneliness an epidemic — with health risk comparable to smoking 15 cigarettes a day and a 29% higher risk of heart disease.[4]

AI

Companion use is climbing fast — and peer-reviewed 2024–25 research warns of a displacement effect: emotional energy meant for people gets redirected to an artificial relationship instead.[19][22]

The market is full of products designed to keep you talking to the AI. Almost none are designed to push you back toward the people in your life.

02 · The insight

Spending on people you love is the most reliable way money buys happiness — and an AI already knows who they are.

Decades of research (Dunn, Aknin, Norton) show prosocial spending beats spending on yourself — and the effect is strongest when the gift goes to someone close, chosen freely, with visible impact.[1][2][6] That is the exact shape of every Everest ALT transaction.

The unlock

Your companion already knows your wife's birthday is Thursday and that your brother just got the job. The noticing is solved. The only missing piece is a safe way to turn that noticing into something that arrives at a door.

06 · How it connects

Two integration tiers — and we're honest about which is which.

Some platforms expose genuine tool use, so the companion can trigger a send directly. Many don't yet. The person's 60-second experience is identical either way.

Direct tool use

The companion sends

On platforms with function-calling / tool use — OpenAI, Anthropic (incl. MCP), xAI — the AI calls Everest ALT directly, inside a conversation, within budget.

Assisted

Everest ALT sends

On apps without a developer hook — Replika, Character.AI, most others — the companion surfaces the moment and Everest ALT's own scheduler completes the send, with your approval rules.

Deepening direct integrations is a core roadmap priority — and the natural door to platform partnerships. Full technical detail, with the actual API calls, is in How the system works.

09 · Why we win

The only growth story in this category that points the same way as its social impact.

Incentives aligned outward

Most companion products profit by maximizing time on the AI — structurally exposed to the displacement risk. We profit only when the AI points a person toward a real human. Growth and stated impact reinforce each other.

Neutral infrastructure

We don't pick a companion winner. Whichever platform wins conversation, they still need a rail to the physical world — and a trusted budget-control and fulfillment layer is hard to build and easy to license.

Trust as moat

Agent-initiated spend demands spend controls, approval thresholds, and fraud monitoring from day one. Doing this credibly is a barrier — and exactly what a platform partner won't want to build themselves.

Specific, defensible tone

60-second setup, a hard cap, a gift from a maker within a defined radius. Falsifiable claims — not "seamless" and "magical" — which is what earns trust in a scrutinized category.
