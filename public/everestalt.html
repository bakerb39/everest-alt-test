05Questions

What people ask us first.

Is this a subscription?

Yes. Everest ALT is $19.99 a month, and your first 7 days are free. That covers the platform itself. Gifts are separate — you only pay for what your agent actually sends, on top of your budget.

Can I stop a gift before it ships?

Yes. Every send shows up in your history the moment it triggers. You have a window to cancel before it goes to fulfillment.

Does my AI companion ever see my payment information?

No. Your companion only ever sees the budget number. Payment details stay with Everest ALT and never touch the agent.

I don't have an AI companion yet. Can I still use this?

Yes. Everest ALT works with the companion or assistant you already talk to. If you don't have one yet, you can still set up occasions and a budget, and send gifts yourself.

What happens if my companion tries to spend past my budget?

Nothing happens. The cap is hard. Once the budget is used, no more sends go through until you raise it or the next cycle starts.

Does my companion place the order itself, or does something else do it?

It depends on the platform — and this is the part we're most careful to be honest about. Some AI platforms support genuine tool use, where a companion can trigger an outside service directly; many consumer companion apps don't expose that to third parties yet. Where that direct integration exists, your companion triggers the gift itself. Where it doesn't, your companion still notices the moment and brings it up, and Everest ALT's own system carries out the send — automatically or with a quick confirmation from you, based on what you've approved. Either way, the result on the doorstep is the same. Deepening these direct integrations is a core part of our roadmap.
