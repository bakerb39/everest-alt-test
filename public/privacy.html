Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 31, 2026
This is a draft written to be clear and honest about what we actually collect today, while Everest ALT is a waitlist. It is not legal advice. Have a lawyer review this before it goes live, especially once the product is handling budgets and payments.

What we collect

Right now, Everest ALT collects one thing: the email address you give us when you join the waitlist. If you use the referral link feature, we also store which email referred which other email, so we can credit you for moving up the list.

We do not collect payment information, financial details, or information about any AI companion or assistant you use, because the product has not launched yet.

How we use it

  • To let you know when Everest ALT opens for early access
  • To track referral positions on the waitlist
  • To occasionally share meaningful updates about what we're building

We do not sell your email address. We do not share it with advertisers.

Where it's stored

Your email is stored on our own hosting infrastructure. If we use a third-party service to help run the waitlist (for example, a hosting or form provider), that service only has access to what's needed to operate the waitlist, nothing more.

Your choices

You can ask us to remove your email from the waitlist at any time by writing to leadership@everestalt.com. We'll remove it within a reasonable time.

When this changes

Once Everest ALT is live and connecting to AI companions, handling budgets, and processing gift orders, this policy will need to expand significantly to cover payment data, third-party AI platform connections, and gift recipient information. We'll update this page and note the date above when that happens.

Contact

Questions about this policy can go to leadership@everestalt.com.
